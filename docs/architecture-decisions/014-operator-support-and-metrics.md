# Operator support and measurement

Verified explicit `OPERATOR_USER_IDS` access is independent of wedding membership. Routine operations show IDs, counts, limits and delivery states, excluding guest answers, file contents and credentials. Privileged changes require recent authentication and an audited reason. Processed-media inspection uses a specific ten-minute grant with an audit on each read. Public reports persist privately without sending email.

Per-workspace controls use optimistic versions; public holds do not revoke private RSVP or admission. Renewal preserves operator restrictions. Account recovery requires independently verified ownership and cannot restore revoked membership or pre-recovery credentials. Durable recovery ledger replay quarantines credentials predating the recovery watermark, including after restoring a backup.

Eight deduplicated funnel events retain only pseudonymous workspace/entity identifiers and timestamps. Cohort activation uses recorded events, not fabricated historical activity. Estimated AI cost and storage usage are operational measurements, not invoices or a completed pilot.
