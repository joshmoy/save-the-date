# Guest management and private RSVP

Use the Harbour app shell for the couple's guest dashboard and a bounded, quiet form layout for household RSVP. Name each household attendee/allocated slot and show its distinct unanswered/pending/confirmed/declined/waitlisted state. Event headings identify forms, keeping mobile submit labels short. Use shared content gutters and labelled controls; no guest-list search exists on the public website.

Owner actions separate guest creation, event eligibility, grant rotation, recipient review and mail queueing. Explain that group labels do not grant access and that rotating a household invitation invalidates previous sessions. CSV preview requires explicit duplicate decisions and reports row outcomes. Existing-household selection hides the irrelevant new-name field. Sensitive answers have explicit owner-only viewing/export controls and a deletion policy control.

The guest page explains who sees optional answers, bounded plus ones, capacity/manual approval, deadlines and the independence of saved confirmation from email delivery. Keep credentials out of normal website URLs and verification captures. Desktop/360px evidence and the two resolved finish-review findings are recorded in verification/guests/report.md.
